## My Career Plan: Setting Goals and Mapping a Pathway
### Grade 7 · CDOS & Career Readiness · NYS CDOS Standards 1 & 2 (Intermediate) · 50 Minutes

---

### NYS-Aligned Standards

**CDOS Standard 1 (Intermediate)** — Relating interests/aptitudes to career fields and building a tentative career plan.
**CDOS Standard 2** — Applying academic skills to career and life decisions.
*NYS Learning Standards for Career Development and Occupational Studies*

---

### Learning Objectives — "I Can" Statements

- I can connect my interests and strengths to a career field.
- I can write a short-term and a long-term career-related goal.
- I can identify the education or training a career pathway requires.

---

### Essential Question

What steps can I start taking now to move toward a career that fits my interests and strengths?

---

### Lesson Sequence

#### Hook / Warm-Up (8 min)
"Five years / ten years" quick-write: Where do you imagine yourself? What kind of work might you enjoy? No wrong answers.

#### Direct Instruction (12 min)
1. Review **interests, aptitudes, abilities** and how they point toward career fields.
2. Introduce a simple **career pathway**: interest → field → required education/training → entry job → growth.
3. Model writing a SMART-style short-term goal (this year) and a long-term goal (after high school).

#### Guided Practice (18 min)
Students complete a **Career Plan Map**:
- A career field that interests me
- Why it fits my strengths
- Education/training it requires
- One short-term goal (this year)
- One long-term goal

Teacher conferences with students as they work.

#### Share & Feedback (8 min)
Partners exchange plans and ask one clarifying question each.

#### Closure (4 min)
Exit ticket: "One action I can take this month toward my goal is ___."

---

### SDI & Differentiation Block

#### Supports for MLLs/ELLs

**Transitioning/Expanding (NYSESLAT Levels 3–4):**
- Provide the Career Plan Map as a sentence-frame template
- Vocabulary: interest, aptitude, pathway, training, goal

**Expanding/Commanding (NYSESLAT Levels 5–6):**
- Extension: research one real program/credential for the chosen pathway
- Encourage precise career vocabulary

#### Supports for Students with IEPs

**SDI Adaptation Dimensions: content, methodology, delivery**

- **Content:** Focus on one career field and one short-term goal
- **Methodology:** Teacher-guided completion; provide a model map; connect to transition planning where an IEP includes it
- **Delivery:** Read aloud; allow scribed/verbal responses; extended time

**Note:** For students with IEP transition plans, this resource can support (not replace) the documented transition goals required for students age 15+ under NYS regulations.

**Suggested Placement:** ICT, Resource Room

### Model Response (excerpt)

"Career field: health care. Why it fits: I like helping people and I'm good at science. Training: a nursing program after high school. Short-term goal: earn a B or higher in science this year. Long-term goal: become a registered nurse."
