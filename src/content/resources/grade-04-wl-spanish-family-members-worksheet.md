## La Familia: Family Members and Descriptions in Spanish
### Grade 4 · World Languages · NYS WL Standard Novice.CLL.2 · Worksheet (35 min)

*Teacher Note: Pair with a family-tree drawing for a presentational extension.*

---

### NYS-Aligned Standard

**Novice.CLL.2** — Understand and use frequent words, phrases, and simple sentences on familiar topics.
*NYS Learning Standards for World Languages (2021)*

---

### Student Directions

**Nombre (Name):** ________________________ **Fecha (Date):** ____________

You are learning to talk about *la familia* (the family) in Spanish.

---

### PARTE 1 — Match the Words

Match the Spanish family word to its English meaning:
| Español | | English |
|---------|--|---------|
| 1. madre | _____ | a. father |
| 2. padre | _____ | b. brother |
| 3. hermano | _____ | c. mother |
| 4. hermana | _____ | d. grandmother |
| 5. abuela | _____ | e. sister |

---

### PARTE 2 — Complete the Sentence

Fill in with a family word in Spanish:
1. Mi (my) ___________ se llama María. (mother)
2. Mi ___________ es alto. (brother)
3. Tengo una ___________. (sister)

---

### PARTE 3 — Describe with Adjectives

Use one adjective (alto/a = tall, bajo/a = short, simpático/a = nice) to describe a family member:
"Mi ___________ es ___________."

---

### PARTE 4 — Mi Familia (Draw & Label)

Draw three family members and label each in Spanish (madre, padre, hermano, etc.). Then say one sentence aloud to a partner: "Esta es mi ___________."

---

### SDI & Differentiation Block

#### Supports for MLLs/ELLs

*Note: For students who are also English language learners, connect Spanish family words to home-language equivalents and pictures.*

**Entering/Emerging (NYSESLAT Levels 1–2):**
- Picture-word cards for each family member; matching by drawing lines
- Allow labeling with picture support only

**Transitioning/Expanding (NYSESLAT Levels 3–4):**
- Sentence frame: "Mi ___ se llama ___."
- Word bank with pictures

#### Supports for Students with IEPs

**SDI Adaptation Dimensions: content, methodology, delivery**

- **Content:** Reduce to 4 family words; complete Parts 1 and 4 only
- **Methodology:** Use family photos; model one labeled example
- **Delivery:** Read aloud; allow verbal/drawn responses; extended time

**Suggested Placement:** ICT, Resource Room

---

### Answer Key

**Parte 1:** 1-c, 2-a, 3-b, 4-e, 5-d
**Parte 2:** 1. madre  2. hermano  3. hermana
*Parts 3 and 4 are open response — evaluate for correct family vocabulary and a complete sentence.*
